# Welcome to Rustsidian

This is a sample vault. A vault is just a folder of Markdown files on your disk, so everything here is yours to edit, move or delete.

## Five things to try

- [ ] Press **Ctrl+N** to create a note
- [ ] Press **Ctrl+O** and type a few letters to jump to any note
- [ ] Press **Ctrl+P** to search every command
- [ ] Click a link: [[Markdown basics]] or [[Links and the graph]]
- [ ] Press **Ctrl+G** to see how your notes connect

> [!tip] Finding your way around
> The strip on the far left opens the file list, search, the graph and settings. Hover over an icon and the bar at the bottom tells you what it does.

## Next

- [[Keyboard shortcuts]] lists the keys worth learning first.
- Tabs close with the **x** on the tab, a middle click, or **Ctrl+W**. **Ctrl+Shift+T** brings the last one back.
- To use your own notes, click the vault icon at the bottom of the left strip and choose **Open folder as vault**.
